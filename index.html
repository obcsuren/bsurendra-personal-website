A BRIEF INTRODUCTION

About Me

Banking professional with a continuing interest in employee welfare and association activities.

I am B. Surendra, serving as Branch Manager at Gowliguda Branch, State Bank of India, Hyderabad.

Alongside my banking responsibilities, I serve as Deputy General Secretary – Headquarters of the SBI OBC Employees Welfare Association, Hyderabad Circle.

My involvement in welfare association activities began in 2011. Earlier, I served as Regional Secretary, Banjara Hills Region, for the SBI Officers Association.

Through these responsibilities, I value constructive engagement, employee welfare, mutual support and the importance of keeping members informed and connected.
